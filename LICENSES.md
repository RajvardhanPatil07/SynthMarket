# Third-Party and Data Licensing Notes

SynthMarket is released under the MIT License in `LICENSE`.

## Data

Market data fetched from external providers is **not** relicensed by SynthMarket. Users are responsible for complying with the terms, licenses, attribution requirements, redistribution limits, and commercial-use restrictions of the selected data provider.

The repository does not ship proprietary historical market datasets.

## Models and dependencies

Third-party Python packages retain their own licenses. Deployments should generate a dependency/software bill of materials and review the licenses of all optional providers before redistribution.
